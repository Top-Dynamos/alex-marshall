042024 and 2025

The hardest lessons.

He hired people he cared about, friends and family among them, to give them a real opportunity.

As the business grew, several left to chase a bigger share or build businesses of their own. He understood the desire. What they did not always see were the costs behind a quality operation: materials, products, employees, office space, technology, overhead and customer service.

When several people leave at once, the pressure is real. He rebuilt the team, kept serving customers, and the business survived. He does not regret helping people. He has changed how he does it.

  1. Revenue is not profit.

    Sustainable building takes patience and discipline.

  2. Trust has to be earned.

    Opportunity comes with responsibility. Generosity needs boundaries.

  3. Build what outlasts any one person.

    Systems, standards and culture keep a company moving when people change direction.

Some people are meant to stay for the entire journey, and others are meant to be part of a chapter.
